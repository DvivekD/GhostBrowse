## 🎬 3-Minute Live Pitch Script

You get slapped with cookie consent banners, 25-field interactive forms, dynamic pricing traps, and hidden baggage fees. You open 8 tabs across Google Flights, Skyscanner, and Kayak, manually cross-checking numbers.*

### Act 3: The Cryptographic Proof (2:00 - 2:30)
> *"Now, here is the secret sauce that makes GhostBrowser production-ready:*
>
> *AI agents hallucinate all the time. If an AI tells you a flight is ₹2,850 when it's actually ₹4,500, users lose money.*
>
> *GhostBrowser solves this with our **W3C C14N 4-Leaf Merkle Tree**. Notice this glowing badge. If I click it, the browser's native **Web Crypto API** inspects 4 cryptographic leaves:*
> - *Leaf A: Verified Price Claim (`₹2,850`)*
> - *Leaf B: Airline & Flight Number (`IndiGo 6E-243`)*
> - *Leaf C: Canonical Itinerary (`BLR → COK at 06:15 AM`)*
> - *Leaf D: Normalized DOM Extract stripped of volatile trackers.*
>
> *If a single byte on the target airline's DOM was altered or hallucinated, the SHA-256 root breaks immediately. We provide **100% cryptographic proof of ground truth**."*
